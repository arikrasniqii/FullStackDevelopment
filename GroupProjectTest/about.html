Kosovo's only international airport, connecting Pristina to the world since 1965.

Our Story

Prishtina International Airport Adem Jashari is the sole gateway for air travel into Kosovo. Originally opened in 1965, the airport was renamed in 2010 in honor of Adem Jashari, founder of the Kosovo Liberation Army. A modern terminal, control tower and expanded infrastructure were completed in 2013, raising the airport's capacity from 2 to 5 million passengers a year.
